●  Questions

What is an AI assistant caller?

An AI assistant that calls a business on behalf of a customer, to ask about services, check hours and availability, or book a visit. Google, Meta, and others already offer this.

What goes wrong when an AI assistant calls a business today?

Most businesses are not ready. The assistant reaches voicemail, gets wrong hours from a stale listing, or is told to call back later. It then reports the next company on the list to the customer instead.

Does the desk pretend to be a person?

No. The desk says it is an automated assistant at the start of every call, and every call is recorded.

What if the assistant has no authority to book?

The desk takes the details and sends you the summary like any other call.

What happens when a customer calls my restoration company after hours?

Six short questions, in the same order every time: name, address, what happened, how long ago, safety, and insurance carrier.
