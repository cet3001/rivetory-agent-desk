●  Questions

Short answers.

What do I get during the pilot?

After-hours and overflow answering, emergency alerts to your on-call tech, booking and owner summaries, with daily summaries during the pilot.

How do I start?

Book a 30-minute setup call on the start-a-pilot page. The desk is set up for you in that call.

What happens at the end of the 14 days?

You pick Core at $297 a month or Pro at $497 a month, or you stop and owe nothing.

Does the guarantee apply after the pilot?

Yes. If the desk does not capture at least one job worth more than the monthly fee in your first 30 paid days, the next month is free.

Can I hear the desk before the pilot?

Yes. Call the demo line at (208) 510-0776 and listen to the desk answer as a sample restoration company.

Is there a limit on calls during the pilot?

No limit is set at launch. A monthly call allowance per plan will be set once real call costs are measured, and existing customers will be told before anything changes.
