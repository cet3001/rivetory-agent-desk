●  How it works

One call, handled. You hear about it in a minute.

Call comes in → Desk answers → Job booked → Summary sent

  1. Step 1

    We pick up.

    Calls forward to the desk when your line is busy or after hours. A person or an AI assistant gets a calm, short intake, not a voicemail.

  2. Step 2

    We book the job.

    Name, address, what happened, water or fire, how long ago, insurance carrier. Emergencies go straight to your on-call tech.

  3. Step 3

    You get the summary.

    A text and an email within a minute, with everything the caller said and the booking. You wake up to jobs, not missed calls.

●  Questions

Short answers.

What does the desk do?

It answers your after-hours and overflow calls, takes the job details, books the visit or alerts your on-call tech for an emergency, and sends you a summary by text and email within a minute.

What is the free pilot?

Fourteen days with the full desk, set up for you in a 30-minute call. No card is needed to start. After the pilot you pick Core at $297 a month or Pro at $497 a month, or you stop and owe nothing.

Does the desk handle calls from AI assistants?

Yes. When an AI assistant calls on behalf of a customer, the desk answers it with your verified facts, service area, hours and emergency availability, and books the job if the assistant has authority to book.
