●  What we need from you

Five things, and the call is done.

  • Your service area and the towns you cover
  • Your hours and your after-hours rule
  • Your on-call tech's name and number
  • How you want jobs booked: a visit time, a callback window, or both
  • Where summaries should go: your mobile number and email

That is the whole setup. If you are not sure about any of it, we decide it together on the call.
