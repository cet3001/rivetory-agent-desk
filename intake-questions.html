●  Answers

What questions does the desk ask a caller?

Six short questions, in the same order every time: name and callback number, address of the damage, what happened, how long ago it started, whether water is still running or anyone is unsafe, and insurance carrier if they know it.

Questions6, always in the same order.

What the caller does not doRepeat anything in the morning. The details go straight to the tech and the owner.

●  Questions

Short answers.

Why these questions, in this order?

They are enough to send a tech. Name and number first, then where the damage is, then what happened and how urgent it is, then the insurance carrier so the file starts clean.

What if the caller does not know their insurance carrier?

The desk asks if they know it. If they do not, the call moves on; the carrier is captured when it is known.

Does the desk ask about price?

No. The desk does not quote a price. It takes the details and books the visit, so the company gives the real quote.

What happens after the questions?

Routine jobs get a visit booked. Active water or fire goes straight to your on-call tech, by call and text, right now. You get a summary within a minute either way.

Does the caller hear a script?

No. The desk does not pitch, does not read a script at the caller, and does not keep them on the phone. It takes the details and tells them what happens next.

What if the caller is an AI assistant?

The desk recognizes it and answers from your verified fact sheet, then books the job if the assistant has authority to book.

What happens when a customer calls my restoration company after hours?

Your line forwards to the desk when you do not pick up. The desk answers, takes the job details, and sends you a summary within a minute.

What happens when a caller reports active water or fire?

The desk confirms it in the first minute, calls and texts your on-call tech immediately, and tells the caller what to do until the tech arrives.

How does the desk handle calls from AI assistants?

The desk recognizes the caller as an AI assistant, answers with facts from your verified fact sheet, and books the job if the assistant has authority to book.
