●  Terms

Terms, in plain words.

Last updated November 21, 2026. Short version: the pilot is free, the plans are month to month, and the guarantee is real.

The service.

Rivetory runs an after-hours and overflow call desk for your company. It answers forwarded calls, takes job details, books visits or alerts your on-call tech for emergencies, and sends you a summary of every call by text and email.

The free pilot.

The pilot runs 14 days from the day the desk is set up. It includes the full desk: answering, emergency alerts, booking and owner summaries, with daily summaries during the pilot. No card is needed to start. At the end of the pilot you pick a plan, or the service stops and you owe nothing.

Plans and payment.

Core is $297 per month. Pro is $497 per month. Plans are month to month with no minimum term. Payment is due at the start of each paid month. Prices are in US dollars.

The guarantee.

If the desk does not capture at least one job worth more than the monthly fee in your first 30 paid days, the next month is free. A captured job is a call the desk handled that became a booked job. Tell us before the next month is billed and we apply the credit.

Cancellation.

Cancel any time. The desk keeps running until the end of the paid month, then stops. There is no cancellation fee. Call forwarding is yours to turn off at any time.

Call limits.

No monthly call limit is set at launch. A monthly call allowance per plan may be set once real call costs are measured. Existing customers will be told before any change takes effect, and the change will not apply to a month already paid for.

Recording and disclosure.

Calls handled by the desk are recorded. The desk announces at the start of every call that it is an automated assistant answering for your company and that the call is recorded. You agree to let the desk make that announcement and recording on your behalf, and to tell your staff that calls forwarded to the desk are recorded.

What the desk does not do.

The desk does not promise new leads, rankings or a number of jobs. It answers the calls your business already receives. It does not give legal, medical or insurance advice, and it does not quote prices for your company unless your fact sheet says so. Emergency guidance given to a caller is general safety guidance, not a substitute for emergency services: a caller in immediate danger is told to call 911.

Your responsibilities.

Keep your fact sheet accurate: services, area, hours, emergency rules and the on-call tech's number. Tell us when any of it changes. Keep your call forwarding set up as agreed in the setup call. Answer the summaries and the emergency alerts the desk sends you.

Liability.

Rivetory is liable only up to the amount you paid in the month the problem happened. Rivetory is not liable for jobs lost to a phone outage, a forwarding failure on your side, or a caller who hangs up before the desk can take the details.

Changes.

If these terms change, the date at the top changes with it. Customers are told before a change that affects their plan, price or the guarantee takes effect.
