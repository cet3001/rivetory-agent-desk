●  Questions

Short answers.

What does the free pilot include?

Fourteen days with the full desk: after-hours and overflow answering, emergency alerts, booking and owner summaries, with daily summaries during the pilot. Setup is done for you in a 30-minute call. No card is needed to start.

What is the difference between Core and Pro?

Core is the desk every night and every overflow call, with owner summaries, emergency escalation, and a weekly recap. Pro adds your verified fact sheet and public facts page, AI-assistant caller handling, a weekly report with call detail, and priority changes the same day.

Is there a setup fee?

No. There is no setup fee, no per-call billing at launch, and no minimum term.

What happens after the pilot?

You pick Core at $297 a month or Pro at $497 a month, or you stop and owe nothing. Plans are month to month.

What is the guarantee?

If the desk does not capture at least one job worth more than the monthly fee in your first 30 paid days, the next month is free. A captured job is a call the desk handled that became a booked job.

Is there a limit on calls?

No limit is set at launch. A monthly call allowance per plan will be set once real call costs are measured, and existing customers will be told before anything changes.
