●  Questions

Short answers.

What does the summary include?

The caller's number, what happened, and the booking. For an emergency, the tech alert goes first.

What about calls that do not become jobs?

You still get the summary, and a weekly recap covers every call, so you can see what the desk caught and what it missed.

Do I get a summary during the pilot too?

Yes. You get daily summaries during the pilot, then a weekly recap on Core and Pro.

What does the weekly recap show?

Every call in one place. Pro adds call detail in a weekly report.

What if the caller is an AI assistant?

You get the summary like any other call.

Are calls recorded?

Yes. Every call is recorded, and the desk says it is an automated assistant and that the call is recorded at the start of every call.

What happens when a customer calls my restoration company after hours?

Your line forwards to the desk when you do not pick up. The desk answers, takes the job details, and sends you a summary within a minute.

What questions does the desk ask a caller?

Six short questions, in the same order every time: name, address, what happened, how long ago, safety, and insurance carrier.

What happens when a caller reports active water or fire?

The desk confirms it in the first minute, calls and texts your on-call tech immediately, and tells the caller what to do until the tech arrives.
