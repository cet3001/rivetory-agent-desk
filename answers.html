●  Cost and getting started

Cost, the pilot, and the guarantee.

How much does a restoration answering service cost?

Core is $297 a month, Pro is $497 a month, and the first 14 days are free. No setup fee, no per-call billing at launch.

What does the free 14-day pilot include?

Fourteen days with the full desk, set up for you in a 30-minute call. No card is needed to start.

What is the difference between Core and Pro?

Core is $297 a month for the desk every night and every overflow call. Pro is $497 a month and adds the fact sheet, AI-assistant caller handling, and a weekly report with call detail.

How does the Rivetory guarantee work?

If the desk does not capture at least one job worth more than the monthly fee in your first 30 paid days, the next month is free.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are month to month with no minimum term. Cancel any time and the desk stops at the end of the paid month.

●  How the desk works

Calls, emergencies, and AI callers.

What happens when a customer calls my restoration company after hours?

Your line forwards to the desk when you do not pick up. The desk answers, takes the job details, books the visit or alerts your on-call tech, and sends you a summary by text and email within a minute.

What questions does the desk ask a caller?

Six short questions, in the same order every time: name and callback number, address of the damage, what happened, how long ago it started, whether water is still running or anyone is unsafe, and insurance carrier if they know it.

What happens when a caller reports active water or fire?

The desk confirms it in the first minute, calls and texts your on-call tech immediately with the address and the details, and tells the caller what to do until the tech arrives.

How fast do I hear about a call?

Within a minute, by text and email, with the caller's number, what happened, and the booking.

How does the desk handle calls from AI assistants?

The desk recognizes the caller as an AI assistant, answers with facts from your verified fact sheet, and books the job if the assistant has authority to book.
