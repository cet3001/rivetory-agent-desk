●  Questions

Short answers.

What does Core include?

Everything in the pilot, plus owner summaries by text and email, emergency escalation day and night, and a weekly recap of every call.

What does Pro add?

Your verified fact sheet and public facts page, AI-assistant caller handling, a weekly report with call detail, and priority changes the same day.

What is a verified fact sheet?

Your services, area, hours, and emergency rules in one place. The desk, your website, and your listings all read from it. Pro also gives you a public facts page, like the sample on the demo page.

Which plan should I start with?

Start with the free 14-day pilot, which is the full desk. After the pilot, Core fits a company that wants every call answered. Pro fits a company that also wants to be ready for AI assistants calling.

Can I switch plans later?

Plans are month to month with no minimum term, so you are not locked into a plan. Pro includes priority changes the same day.

Is the guarantee the same on both plans?

Yes. If the desk does not capture at least one job worth more than the monthly fee in your first 30 paid days, the next month is free, on either plan.
