How does the Rivetory guarantee work?

If the desk does not capture at least one job worth more than the monthly fee in your first 30 paid days, the next month is free. A captured job is a call the desk handled that became a booked job.

●  Questions

Short answers.

What is a captured job?

A call the desk handled that became a booked job.

Does the pilot count toward the 30 days?

The 30 days are paid days, after the free 14-day pilot.

What does the desk have to do?

The desk has to answer the call and book the job. The guarantee counts booked jobs, not calls that were only answered.

What happens after the first month?

The guarantee covers the first 30 paid days. Plans stay month to month after that, and you can cancel any time.

How do I know if the desk hit the bar?

The weekly recap shows every call and what it became, so the count is on the record. If the desk did not capture a job worth more than the monthly fee, the next month is free.

Does the guarantee cost extra?

No. It is part of the plan, at the same monthly price.
