●  How it works

What happens when a call comes in.

Your line forwards to the desk when you do not pick up. From there it is four short moves, and you hear about it within a minute.

Call comes in → Desk answers → Job booked → Summary sent

  1. Step 1

    The desk answers.

    It says it is Rivetory, an automated assistant answering for your company, and that the call is recorded. Then it asks short questions and listens.

  2. Step 2

    The desk takes the job.

    Name, address, what happened, water or fire, how long ago, insurance carrier. Enough to send a tech, nothing the caller has to repeat.

  3. Step 3

    The desk books or escalates.

    Routine jobs get a visit booked. Active water or fire goes straight to your on-call tech, by call and text, right now.

  4. Step 4

    You get the summary.

    A text and an email within a minute, with the caller's number, what happened and the booking. A weekly recap covers every call.

●  A person calling

Calm, short, and to the point.

Someone calling at 2am is stressed. The desk does not pitch, does not read a script at them and does not keep them on the phone. It takes the details, tells them what happens next, and tells them what to do until the tech arrives.

The caller hears a real plan: a visit time, a tech on the way for an emergency, or a callback window in the morning.

●  An AI assistant calling

Fast, factual, and from your fact sheet.

When an assistant calls on behalf of a customer, it wants facts, not a conversation. The desk answers from your verified fact sheet: service area, hours, emergency availability, response time.

If the assistant has authority to book, the desk books the job. If it does not, the desk takes the details and sends you the summary like any other call.

●  What you receive

Every call, in your pocket.

  • A summary within a minute. Text and email, with the caller's number, what happened and the booking.
  • An emergency alert that cannot wait. Your on-call tech is called and texted the moment active water or fire is confirmed.
  • A weekly recap. Every call in one place, so you can see what the desk caught and what it missed.
  • One fact sheet. Your services, area, hours and emergency rules live in one place, and the desk, your website and your listings all read from it.

Recording and disclosure are not optional. The desk announces it is an automated assistant and that the call is recorded, at the start of every call.
