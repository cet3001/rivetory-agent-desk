●  Answers

What happens when a customer calls my restoration company after hours?

Your line forwards to the desk when you do not pick up. The desk answers, takes the job details, books the visit or alerts your on-call tech, and sends you a summary by text and email within a minute.

When the desk picks upAfter hours and on overflow, whenever you do not answer.
What the caller hearsA calm greeting that says the call is answered by Rivetory, an automated assistant, and that the call is recorded.
What you getA text and email summary within a minute, with the caller's number, what happened, and the booking.

●  Questions

Short answers.

What does the desk ask the caller?

Name, address, what happened, water or fire, how long ago, and insurance carrier. Enough to send a tech, nothing the caller has to repeat in the morning.

What happens if it is an emergency?

Active water or fire goes straight to your on-call tech. The desk calls and texts the tech immediately with the address and the details, and books the first visit.

What happens to a call that is not an emergency?

Routine jobs get a visit booked. Non-emergencies get a booked visit or a morning callback, not a 2am wake-up.

Does the desk work on weekends?

The desk answers after hours and on overflow. Your line forwards to it whenever you do not pick up, including weekends.

What do I hear about first?

Emergencies go to your on-call tech first, by call and text. You get the summary within a minute either way.

Can I hear the desk work before I sign up?

Yes. Call the demo line at (208) 510-0776 and listen to the desk answer as a sample restoration company.
