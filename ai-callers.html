●  The new caller

Your next caller might not be a person.

A customer asks their assistant to find an emergency water damage company, check the hours and book the visit. That assistant picks up the phone and calls you. What it meets decides whether you get the job.

Who is calling.

Google, Meta and others now let a customer send an AI assistant to call a business for them. It announces itself, asks short questions and reports back to the customer. It is already used for restaurants, salons and appointments, and it is moving into home services.

Most owners have never heard one call. That is the point. The first time it happens to your company, it will sound like a strange, fast, polite caller who wants facts and a booking.

What goes wrong today.

  • Voicemail. The assistant cannot leave a useful message and cannot wait. It moves on.
  • Wrong hours. A stale listing says you are closed, so the assistant reports that to the customer.
  • A competitor first. The assistant lists the companies it can reach. If it cannot reach you, it books the one it can.
  • No booking. Even when it reaches a person, most staff do not know what the caller is or what to do with it.

●  How the desk handles it

Fast, factual, and from your fact sheet.

  1. 1

    Recognized.

    The desk recognizes the caller as an AI assistant from how it announces itself.

  2. 2

    Answered with facts.

    Service area, hours, emergency availability, response time. Straight from your verified fact sheet, the same facts on your public facts page.

  3. 3

    Booked or logged.

    If the assistant has authority to book, the desk books the job. If not, it takes the details and you get the summary like any other call.

AI callers are a second kind of caller, not the product. The product is that every call gets answered, whoever or whatever is calling.

●  The honest part

AI is a touchy subject. The money is not.

Plenty of owners are wary of AI, and that is fair. New technology has burned small businesses before, and nobody wants a robot representing their company.

Here is the plain version. The desk is an assistant that answers the phone when you cannot. It says what it is at the start of every call. It does not pretend to be a person. And the reason to have it is not the technology. It is the job you would have lost to a missed call, and the money that job was worth.

AI is here to stay, for better or worse. The companies that get ready first keep the jobs. The ones that wait lose them to whoever picked up.
